This page republishes the Environmental Protection Department’s Air Quality Health Index and the Hong Kong Observatory’s weather and warnings. It is not medical advice and it is not an official alert tool. GitHub Actions refreshes about once an hour, so figures and warnings can lag the official sites. If you feel unwell, talk to a clinician.
